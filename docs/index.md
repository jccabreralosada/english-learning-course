## Six connected skills

| Study on this site | Practise with your interactive tutor |
|---|---|
| **Grammar:** clear rules and original examples | **Listening:** understand spoken English |
| **Vocabulary:** useful words in context | **Speaking:** express yourself in conversations |
| **Writing:** guided to independent tasks | **Pronunciation:** understandable sounds and rhythm |

All six skills are important. The lessons provide learning outcomes and suggestions for oral practice; the separate **English Learning Program** tutor guides interaction, formal assessments and private progress tracking.

## How to study

1. **Read** the short concept and bilingual clarification.
2. **Check** your understanding before opening explanations or answers.
3. **Practise** grammar, vocabulary and Writing at your own pace; use the suggested pauses.
4. **Speak and listen** with the interactive tutor when you are ready.
5. **Look things up** in [English Quick Reference](reference/index.md) when you need a word or rule without reading a full lesson.

You can explore the [seven course levels](curriculum/index.md). Lessons at later levels will appear progressively; the level descriptions are not evidence of completed coursework.

The course follows the topic sequence of *English File Fourth Edition* where verified, but all public educational material is original. This site is not affiliated with Oxford University Press.
