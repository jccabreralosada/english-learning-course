# English Learning Program

Welcome to an original learning program designed to develop **Grammar, Vocabulary, Writing, Listening, Speaking and Pronunciation** together.

!!! info "Start learning"
    **Elementary 1A** and **Elementary 1B** are available with original explanations, guided practice, answer keys, and activities for the interactive tutor.

## How the program works

- **Read and review:** learn grammar and vocabulary with bilingual central concepts, examples, purposeful visuals and original self-study activities.
- **Practice interactively:** use the separate ChatGPT learning project for listening, speaking, pronunciation and assessments.
- **Review cumulatively:** revisit earlier concepts through spaced and interleaved practice; track your private progress separately.
- **Build real-world English:** integrate everyday phrases, modal verbs, phrasal verbs and occasional technology vocabulary.
