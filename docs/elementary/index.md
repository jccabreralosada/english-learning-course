# Elementary

Start building sentences, asking simple questions and communicating in familiar classroom situations. Read the explanations, try original practice exercises and use the tutor for spoken activities.

## Unit 1 — Start here

1. **[1A — Welcome to the class](unit_01/lesson_a.md)** — affirmative *be*, subject pronouns, days and numbers 0–20.
2. **[1B — One world](unit_01/lesson_b.md)** — negative and question forms of *be*, countries and numbers 21–100.
3. **[1C — What's your email?](unit_01/lesson_c.md)** — possessive adjectives, classroom language and spelling.

**Suggested study path:** 1A → 1B → 1C. Each lesson includes small comprehension checks, practice solutions, Writing tasks and suggestions for interactive speaking, listening and pronunciation.

Need just a rule or word? Try [English Quick Reference](../reference/index.md). Additional Elementary lessons will be added progressively.

The site supports independent study. Your tutor handles interactive practice and formal assessments separately.
