# Grammar Rules

Compact patterns for looking up a form. For explanations and practice, open the linked lesson.

## Subject pronouns and present *be*

| Subject | Affirmative | Negative | Question example |
|---|---|---|---|
| I | I am | I am not | Am I ready? |
| you | you are | you are not | Are you ready? |
| he | he is | he is not | Is he ready? |
| she | she is | she is not | Is she ready? |
| it | it is | it is not | Is it ready? |
| we | we are | we are not | Are we ready? |
| they | they are | they are not | Are they ready? |

## Short answers

| Question | Positive | Negative |
|---|---|---|
| Are you ready? | Yes, I am. | No, I'm not. |
| Is she here? | Yes, she is. | No, she isn't. |
| Are they students? | Yes, they are. | No, they aren't. |

## Possessive adjectives

| Subject / owner | Possessive + noun |
|---|---|
| I | my book |
| you | your book |
| he | his book |
| she | her book |
| it | its name |
| we | our book |
| they | their book |

## Quick word order

| Type | Pattern | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Affirmative | subject + be + information | She is here. |
| Negative | subject + be + not + information | She isn't here. |
| Yes/no question | be + subject + information? | Is she here? |
| Origin question | Where + be + subject + from? | Where are you from? |
