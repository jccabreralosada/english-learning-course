# Phrasal Verbs

A small searchable starting list. Phrasal verbs are combinations of a verb and one or more particles; their meaning is not always predictable from the separate words.

| Expression | Español | Brief example |
|---|---|---|
| get up | levantarse | I get up at seven. |
| sit down | sentarse | Sit down, please. |
| turn on | encender | Turn on the light. |
| turn off | apagar | Turn off the light. |

These entries are **look-up vocabulary**, not claims that the associated grammar is covered in Elementary Unit 1. Additional entries will be selected from verified course topics and everyday usage.
