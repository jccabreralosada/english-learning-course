# Confusing Words

Check the difference in meaning or grammar before writing.

| Pair | Difference | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| your / you're | **your** = possessive; **you're** = *you are* | Your book. / You're ready. |
| its / it's | **its** = possessive; **it's** = *it is* | Its name. / It's Monday. |
| their / they're | **their** = possessive; **they're** = *they are* | Their class. / They're here. |
| his / he's | **his** = possessive; **he's** = *he is* in these examples | His book. / He's ready. |
| thirteen / thirty | Different number and spelling | 13 / 30 |

For the underlying rule, see [possessive adjectives](../elementary/unit_01/lesson_c.md).
